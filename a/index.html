      Can I buy property in Dubai if I do not live in the UAE?

      Yes. Overseas buyers can buy freehold property in designated areas of Dubai. Many of my clients buy from the UK and India and complete the whole process remotely, with me handling viewings, paperwork and handover.

      What budget do I need to start?

      It depends on your goal and the area. Share your budget in the Investor Matcher and I will show you what is realistic, including when it is better to wait.

      How do payment plans work on off-plan property?

      Developers usually split the price into instalments during construction, with the balance due at or after handover. Every plan is different, so I map out the cash you need at each stage before you commit.

      Can buying property get me a Golden Visa?

      Property can qualify you for UAE residency when it meets the thresholds set by the authorities. Those rules change, so I confirm the current requirements for your case before you buy.

      How do I know my money is protected?

      For off-plan purchases, payments go into a project escrow account regulated by the Dubai Land Department, not to the developer directly. I also show you how to verify my licence and the project permit yourself.

      Do you only sell new launches?

      No. I recommend what fits your numbers, whether that is off-plan, a ready property or nothing at all for now.
