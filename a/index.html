Who I am

An architect who learned to read the numbers.

I trained in architecture, so I look at a building the way it will be lived in and maintained, not the way it looks in a brochure.

I then specialised in Real Estate Economics and Finance at the London School of Economics. That is where the numbers took over: yield, cash flow, supply and the scenarios behind every decision.

I have advised investors in Dubai since 2021. Today I am a Senior Portfolio Manager at 3X Capital, listed in the Top 1% tier, building portfolios for clients in Dubai and internationally.
